Artisanal Heritage

A Jewellery Story Rooted in Tradition

In South Indian culture, jewellery is more than an accessory — it is an heirloom of blessings, sentiment, and heritage. Sri Lakshmi Jewellers was established in Guntur to celebrate this sacred beauty with modern craftsmanship.

We blend ancient Temple Nakshi carving techniques with modern lightweight casting and 1-gram micro gold polish, giving you the majestic aura of traditional 22k gold with comfortable all-day wearability.
